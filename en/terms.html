1. What Diana is

Diana is an app for practising English by talking out loud with a teacher who is an artificial intelligence. It helps you get comfortable speaking, but it is not an official course, it doesn't award qualifications and it doesn't replace a qualified teacher.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older and have an Apple account. You sign up with "Sign in with Apple" and by accepting the privacy notice.

3. Diana is an AI and can make mistakes

  • What Diana says is written by an artificial intelligence model (Claude, by Anthropic). It can get things wrong in English, in its corrections or in what it says about any topic, and it can misremember something you told it.
  • Don't use what Diana says as medical, legal, psychological or financial advice. If you need that kind of help, talk to a professional. If you are in danger, call 112 (or your local emergency number).
  • Diana is not a person and nobody else is listening: nobody hears your calls.

5. Plans and subscriptions

  • Free: 5 minutes, once.
  • Medium: 25 minutes a week. Complete: 1 hour (60 minutes) a week. The price of each is shown on the Plans screen before you buy.
  • 7-day free trial of the Medium plan: if your Apple account has never had a Diana subscription, you can try Medium free for 7 days, once per Apple account (it is an Apple offer and Apple decides whether you can use it). During the trial you get Medium's 25 minutes for that week. When you start it, your Medium subscription is set up: if you don't cancel, Apple charges you for the first month of Medium when the 7 days end and it keeps renewing every month. To pay nothing, cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends (see how below). If you cancel, the trial lasts until the end of the 7 days and then you move to Free, with any free minutes you had left. Complete has no free trial.
  • Subscriptions are charged monthly through your Apple account and renew automatically until you cancel. Minutes renew every week from the day you subscribed; unused minutes don't carry over to the next week.
  • The real seconds of the call are counted. Time the app spends reconnecting or on hold (for example, if a phone call comes in) is not counted, up to 15 minutes a day; anything beyond that is.
  • Cancelling: in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions (or "Manage subscription" in Plans), at least 24 hours before it renews. You keep your plan until the end of the paid month and then move to Free; Diana still remembers you.
  • If you move to a lower plan, the change takes effect at the end of the month already paid. If you move up from Medium to Complete, Apple makes the change straight away.
  • Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple, under its terms. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Deleting your account does not cancel your subscription. Cancel it with Apple.
